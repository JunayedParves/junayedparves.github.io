    Power BI · Supply Chain

    Nexora Apparels: 20-Year Supply Chain Intelligence

    Problem: Revenue grew 4× in 20 years. Is the group growing profitably while still delivering to buyers on time?

    Key outcomeGrowth hid a delivery crisis: on-time-in-full fell from 63% to 40% and inventory days rose from 16 to 56. Direct material is 78% of cost, making procurement the biggest margin lever.

    Power BI · Pharmacy Retail

    WellCare Pharma: Growth, Profit & Outlook

    Problem: Net sales rose about 24× in four years. Is the growth healthy, and where should management focus next?

    Key outcomeGrowth came from new stores, not better stores, and one key account is 30.5% of sales. Cutting stock cover from 53 to 45 days frees about BDT 14m.

    Excel · Root Cause Analysis

    Project Tollgate: Checkout Conversion Root Cause

    Problem: Checkout completion fell sharply in June and the product team blamed PayPal. What actually broke, and what is it costing?

    Key outcomeA new payment option, PayQuick, caused it: 95% of its checkouts time out. Completion fell from 68.7% to 59.1%, about $71K lost in 13 days. PayPal was ruled out (p = 0.45).

    • Excel
    • Statistical testing
    • Python
