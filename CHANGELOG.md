# Changelog

All notable changes to the **code and design** of this site are listed here.
Content changes (edits in the Google Sheet) are not listed; they are saved
automatically as `content: sync from Google Sheet <date>` commits.

This project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/):

- **MAJOR** (v**2**.0.0) — big redesign or a change that breaks the Sheet format
  (e.g. renamed columns you'd have to fix in the Sheet)
- **MINOR** (v1.**1**.0) — new feature or section, fully backwards compatible
- **PATCH** (v1.0.**1**) — small fixes: typos in code, styling tweaks, bug fixes

## [Unreleased]

## [3.0.0] — 2026-10-31

### Changed — complete redesign
- **Positioning:** rebuilt for Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst and Business Analyst
  roles. The new hero ("Turning Data Into Business Intelligence & Decisions") shows target roles,
  capabilities, résumé, LinkedIn, GitHub and Tableau Public links.
- **Design system:** navy `#0B1220` with sky-blue `#39BDF8` and violet `#8B7CFF` accents, Inter +
  IBM Plex Mono, split into `css/variables.css`, `base.css`, `components.css` and `pages.css`.
- **Architecture:** pages are now pre-built HTML generated by `scripts/build.js` from `data/*.json`
  and `content/case-studies/*.json`, so all content is crawlable without JavaScript. The Google
  Sheet → JSON sync is kept; the sync Action now also rebuilds and commits the pages.
- **Sections:** professional snapshot (stats, role fit, industries), featured case studies with
  working filters (Power BI, SQL, Python, Tableau, Business Analytics, Predictive Analytics),
  technical expertise in four categories, an achievement-led experience timeline with tools and
  impact, education split into completed and in progress, and a Knowledge Hub (tabs) for books,
  podcasts, people and platforms.
- **Contact:** email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Tableau Public and résumé. The contact form only appears once a
  Formspree ID is configured, so there is never a non-working form.
- New share image and favicon.

### Added
- **Projects page** (`/projects/`) with filters, case studies, more projects and Tableau dashboards.
- **Seven case-study pages** (`/projects/<slug>/`): TakaPay credit risk, Nexora supply chain,
  Nabanna retail, WellCare pharmacy, Project Tollgate RCA, Kinun campaign profitability and
  Databel churn. Each has a summary, problem, data, method, KPIs, a screenshot gallery with a
  lightbox, findings, recommendations, limitations and links.
- Real dashboard screenshots for every case study (Databel no longer shows a blank placeholder).
- **SEO:** unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter tags, schema.org
  `Person`, `WebSite`, `CollectionPage`, `CreativeWork` and `BreadcrumbList`, and a generated sitemap.
- `PORTFOLIO_AUDIT.md`: project audit, scores, selection decisions and capability gaps.

### Removed
- Runtime rendering in the browser (`js/script.js`) and the old single stylesheet (`css/style.css`).
- Weaker or broken items: the bank-churn and Pixar experiments, 2023 course exercises, and the
  "this portfolio" card (repositories are untouched on GitHub).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-30

First launch.

### Added
- One-page portfolio: About, Skills, Experience, Education, Projects (with
  filter tabs), Books, Podcasts, People I follow, Résumé & contact, footer.
- All content loaded from a published Google Sheet (one tab per section),
  with automatic fallback to `data/*.json`.
- `show` and `order` columns to hide and reorder anything from the Sheet.
- Empty sections hide themselves; section numbers and colours adjust.
- Loading skeletons, lazy-loaded images, image placeholders.
- Responsive layout (desktop, tablet, 390px phones) with hamburger menu.
- Contact form via Formspree (falls back to email if not configured).
- GitHub Actions: daily Sheet → JSON backup (`sync-content.yml`) and
  GitHub Pages deployment (`deploy.yml`).
- SEO: meta description, Open Graph share image, favicon, sitemap, robots.txt.
- Custom 404 page.
- Template CSVs for every Sheet tab in `templates/`.
